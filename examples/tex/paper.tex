\section{Introduction}
Rooftop vegetation is a well-known passive cooling measure \cite{varga2005}. Field campaigns measured large surface temperature reductions \cite{okafor2011,zhou2021}, and sensor networks now allow estimates at neighbourhood scale \citep{haddad2022}. Green roofs reduce summer cooling energy by about 10\% in humid climates.
