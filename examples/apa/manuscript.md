# Roof vegetation and cooling

Roof vegetation lowers surface temperatures (Okafor & Lindqvist, 2011; Varga, 2005). Haddad and Moreau (2023) combined loggers with simulation. Sedum roofs lose less water in drought (Zhou & Brandt, 2021), and irrigation increases demand (Nakamura et al., 2018).

Savings in humid climates remain uncertain (Moreau, 2019; Haddad & Moreau, 2022).
